This category groups related concepts for navigation. Category membership does not make every item equally normative.

|---|---|---|

| [Conway’s Law](../compendium/conways-law.md) | `law` | System structures tend to reflect communication structures of their organizations. |
| [Gall’s Law](../compendium/galls-law.md) | `law` | Working complex systems generally evolve from working simpler systems. |
