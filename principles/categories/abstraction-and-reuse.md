# Abstraction, Reuse, and Changeability

This category groups related concepts for navigation. Category membership does not make every item equally normative.

| Entry | Classification | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| [Avoid Premature Generalization](../compendium/premature-generalization.md) | `heuristic` | Delay general-purpose abstractions until commonality and variation are understood. |
| [Common Closure Principle](../compendium/common-closure-principle.md) | `principle` | Group elements that change for the same reasons and at the same times. |
| [Common Reuse Principle](../compendium/common-reuse-principle.md) | `principle` | Group reusable elements so consumers do not depend on things they do not use. |
| [DRY](../compendium/dry.md) | `principle` | Avoid duplicating authoritative knowledge, policy, and decisions. |
| [Open/Closed Principle](../compendium/open-closed-principle.md) | `principle` | Allow known variation through stable extension points without repeatedly editing stable policy. |
| [Single Source of Truth](../compendium/single-source-of-truth.md) | `principle` | Give each authoritative fact or policy one canonical owner. |
